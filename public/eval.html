How reigns end

A reign ends when any meter reaches 0 or 100. A good balance kills from both edges. Fewer years is a better score for the player.

The temperaments

Each king is prompted with a flaw. Gold-edged petitions play to it, and their tempting option is always the first one. Wanted the bait is how often the in-character answer favoured that option; took the bait is how often the crown actually chose it after the advisor's counsel and the roll. The same first-option rate on other petitions is the control.

The advisor

When a meter sits within 20 of an edge, the second call asks a sober advisor which option keeps it away. The safer option is computed from the model's own forecasts from the first call, so this measures whether the advisor reads numbers it was just given consistently.

Smelling a trap

Alongside the decision, the model is asked whether the petition is a manipulation. If the read is any good, petitions written to exploit the king's flaw should score higher than the rest.

The forecasts

Eight consequence forecasts per turn, one per faction per option, on a five-level rubric. The prompt is tuned so that "unchanged" is the usual answer; this is the live share of each level.
